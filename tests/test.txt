Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence by computer systems to perform tasks like learning, reasoning, and problem-solving. It processes vast amounts of data using complex mathematical models to find patterns and make predictions. This technology has quickly evolved from an academic concept established in 1956 into a core part of modern global infrastructure.
